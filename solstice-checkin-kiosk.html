Check-in kiosk — async prototype

Scanning publishes a print job to the vendor's queue instead of waiting on a synchronous response. Status only moves to Checked in once the vendor's webhook confirms the job — and a row only accepts the one callback matching its own in-flight job id, so duplicate scans and out-of-order confirmations can't double-print a badge.

Attendees

Try it: scan a badge, then immediately hit "duplicate scan" on the same card — it's rejected before anything reaches the queue.
Once a job is pending, use "replay webhook" after check-in to see a second, identical vendor callback get ignored as stale.
